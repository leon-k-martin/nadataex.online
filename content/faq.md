## production

All designs are created by me and the production takes place in my small studio area in my room. I have never studied fashion design and I am not a seamstress, so please bear this in mind when you buy something from me.

## pricing

Everything I sell is handmade and the prices reflect that. I cannot, nor want to, compete with fast fashion prices. That being said I hope the love that goes into every stitch is recognisable, and this care in creation is reflected in the care with which each item is kept.

## return policy

Due to the nature of these items being 1 of 1 handmade pieces, returns will not be accepted unless there is a mistake on my end. Before making an inquiry about a bag, please make up your mind that you want to buy the product, even if you don't know 100% what it will look like beforehand. Every bag is unique and no two are the same.

## repairing

If your bag breaks within one year and you can no longer carry it, I will try to repair it. This is free of charge for you. This does not apply if it is obvious that the bag has torn/broken due to destructive handling.

After one year, I will have to charge an additional fee in the event of a repair, as this is not included in my prices.

## materials

80% of the materials are second-hand. This means that there may already be signs of use. The tarpaulins I use are not comparable with the "Freitag" tarpaulins as mine are thinner. I try to choose all materials carefully, but I can't always predict the durability/quality with constant use.
